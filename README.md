Website Pages

 Home — `index.html`

The home page introduces Lunar Gaming and displays:

* Featured games
* Game genres
* Gaming statistics
* Upcoming games
* Gaming categories
* Links to other pages

Games can easily be added to the games table.

 About — `about.html`

The About page provides information about:

* Lunar Gaming
* Our story
* Our mission
* Our vision
* Our values
* What we offer

 Services — `services.html`

The Services page displays the gaming services offered by Lunar Gaming, including:

* Game discovery
* Gaming tournaments
* Gaming coaching
* Gaming setup
* Gaming community
* Gaming news

 Enquiry — `enquiry.html`

The enquiry page contains a simple form where visitors can provide:

* Full name
* Email address
* Enquiry type
* Message

 Contact — `contact.html`

The contact page provides:

* Contact information
* Business hours
* Contact form
* Frequently asked questions
