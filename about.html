Our Mission

Lunar Gaming Hub was founded in 2026 with one goal: to give young gamers in Limpopo a place to compete, learn, and grow. We saw too much talent going to waste because there were no local tournaments, no coaching, and no support. So we built a community hub that’s free, safe, and focused on development both in-game and in real life.

What we do:

We host monthly esports tournaments, run free coaching programs, and teach tech skills like PC building and content creation. Whether you’re a casual player or trying to go pro, there’s a place for you here. We believe gaming builds teamwork, problem-solving, and confidence. Our programs are designed to turn passion into opportunity.

Our Values:

  1. Community First: Everyone is welcome. No toxicity, just GG’s.
  2. Growth: We help you level up your skills and your future.
  3. Access: Free programs and low-data website so no one gets left behind.
